# Tableau Visualizations

This folder contains the Tableau workbook used to create the final capstone visualizations.

## Workbook

**Google Data Analytics Professional Capstone Visuals.twbx**

## Core Visuals

The workbook contains the primary visuals used in the final presentation:

1. **Household income vs. laptop use**
   Weighted NTIA 2023 analysis showing a 43.4 percentage-point gap between households earning under $25,000 and households earning $150,000 or more.

2. **Age and internet-based financial-services use**
   Weighted NTIA 2023 analysis comparing adults age 65+ with adults ages 18–64 across household-income groups.

3. **Core-device repair outcomes**
   Open Repair analysis showing fixed, repairable, end-of-life, and unknown outcomes for laptops, desktops, mobile phones, and tablets.

4. **Documented end-of-life repair barriers**
   Open Repair analysis grouping documented barriers into information/tools/access, parts cost/supply, and wear-out categories.

5. **EPA WARM scenario**
   Modeled greenhouse-gas emissions avoided for reuse/source reduction and recycling of 500 portable electronic devices compared with landfill.

6. **Integrated evidence and pilot recommendation dashboard**
   Summary dashboard combining the strongest findings into a decision-oriented recommendation.

## Interpretation Notes

- **Repairable** does not mean a device was successfully repaired during the recorded attempt.
- Repair-barrier percentages apply only to end-of-life records with a documented barrier.
- NTIA measures reported technology and internet use, not digital proficiency.
- WARM outputs are modeled scenario estimates, not observed program outcomes.
