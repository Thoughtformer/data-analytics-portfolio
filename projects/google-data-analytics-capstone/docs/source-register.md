# Source & Evidence Register

This document tracks the datasets, institutional reports, and external evidence used in the capstone project.

Sources are separated into:

- **Primary datasets** — data that will be directly cleaned, queried, analyzed, or visualized as part of the capstone.
- **Supporting sources** — published data, research, reports, or program evaluations used to provide context, validate findings, or evaluate intervention feasibility.

## Evidence Standards

The following standards will be applied throughout the project:

- Primary analytical findings will be distinguished from findings reported by external sources.
- Correlation will not be presented as proof of causation.
- Government, academic, and institutional sources will be preferred where available.
- Publication dates and dataset years will be recorded so findings are not presented outside their appropriate time period.
- Methodological limitations will be documented for evidence used in the final analysis.
- Scenario estimates will be clearly labeled as modeled outcomes rather than observed results.
- Original datasets will not be modified or represented as the author's own data.
- Source and licensing requirements will be reviewed before raw data is republished in the repository.

As sources are incorporated into the project, the register will be expanded to document:

- Publication or dataset year
- Date accessed
- Dataset version
- Relevant variables or metrics
- Geographic coverage
- Population represented
- Sample size, when applicable
- Methodology
- Known limitations
- Licensing or attribution requirements
- Project files using the source
- Final citation
