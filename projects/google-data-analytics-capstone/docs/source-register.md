## Source Metadata

Metadata known at the beginning of the project is documented here. Additional fields will be completed as each source is incorporated into the analysis.

### P01 — NTIA Internet Use Survey

- **Dataset:** November 2023 Internet Use Survey / Computer and Internet Use Supplement
- **Data year:** 2023
- **Release posted:** August 30, 2024
- **Accessed:** September 20, 2026
- **Geographic coverage:** United States
- **Population / scope:** Current Population Survey households; person-level supplement questions apply to household members age 3 and older
- **Approximate survey coverage:** Approximately 44,000 interviewed households
- **Data collection period:** November 12–18, 2023
- **Methodology:** Supplement to the U.S. Census Bureau Current Population Survey
- **Data collection:** Household and person-level survey responses; proxy responses permitted
- **Data processing:** Consistency editing and imputation for eligible missing responses
- **Available formats:** CSV, R, Stata, and fixed-width files
- **Primary project role:** Analyze digital access, device use, internet use, online activity, and demographic relationships
- **Known limitations:** Survey and proxy-response data; estimates require appropriate weighting; missing responses may be imputed; cross-sectional relationships do not establish causation
- **Exact variables used:** To be determined after dataset inspection
- **Project files using source:** To be added during analysis

### P02 — Open Repair Alliance Open Repair Data

- **Dataset:** Full Open Repair Alliance dataset
- **Release / version:** ORDS v0.3
- **Data coverage:** Includes records through July 2025
- **Dataset updated:** October 2025
- **Accessed:** September 20, 2026
- **Geographic coverage:** International
- **Record count:** 305,649 electrical and electronic equipment repair attempts
- **Unit of observation:** Individual repair attempt
- **Methodology:** Repair-event data contributed by Open Repair Alliance partner organizations
- **Primary project role:** Analyze repair outcomes, product categories, product age, and barriers to successful repair
- **Relevant available fields:** Product category, brand, product age, repair status, repair barrier, country, event date, and problem description
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **Known limitations:** Repair-event participants and devices are self-selected and are not representative of all discarded electronics or all households
- **Exact variables used:** To be finalized during analysis
- **Project files using source:** To be added during analysis

- **Accessed:** September 20, 2026
- **Geographic coverage:** United States and supported ACS geographies
- **Population / scope:** U.S. resident population and housing; relevant S2802 estimates describe people living in households

### S02 — NCES PIAAC 2023

- **Dataset / report:** 2023 U.S. Program for the International Assessment of Adult Competencies (PIAAC)
- **Data collection:** 2022–2023
- **Reported as:** 2023 results
- **Accessed:** September 20, 2026
- **Geographic coverage:** United States
- **Population:** Adults ages 16–74 in the U.S. sample
- **Background-questionnaire respondents:** 4,637
- **Assessment respondents:** 4,574
- **Methodology:** Nationally representative adult skills assessment using an interviewer-administered background questionnaire and tablet-based assessment
- **Skills measured:** Literacy, numeracy, and adaptive problem solving
- **Primary project role:** Examine differences in adaptive problem-solving proficiency across demographic groups
- **Known limitations:** U.S. response rates were relatively low; estimates require survey weighting; results identify associations rather than causal relationships
- **Exact measures used:** To be finalized during analysis
