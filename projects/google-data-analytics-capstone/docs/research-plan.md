# Research Plan

## Primary Research Question

Does existing public evidence support the need for an integrated community technology program combining device refurbishment, technical education, digital inclusion, and community support?

## Supporting Questions

### Digital Access

- How does technology access vary by age?
- How does access vary by household income?
- How does access vary by education level?
- How does access vary by employment status?
- Which populations rely more heavily on smartphones rather than full computers?

### Digital Skills

- How does adaptive problem-solving ability vary by age?
- How strongly is digital/problem-solving proficiency associated with education?
- Which populations appear most likely to benefit from personalized technical support?

### Youth Access

- Are lower-income young people less likely to have access to desktop or laptop computers?
- How common is smartphone-only access among economically disadvantaged youth?

### Older Adults and Economic Vulnerability

- How do fraud losses differ by age group?
- Which scam categories disproportionately affect older adults?
- Is there evidence of demand for age-specific technical support?

### Repair and Reuse

- What percentage of devices brought to community repair events are repaired?
- What percentage are considered repairable but remain unrepaired?
- Which device categories have the highest repair success rates?
- How does product age relate to repair outcomes?
- What barriers most commonly prevent repair?

### Environmental Impact

- How much electronic waste is generated?
- What proportion is formally recycled?
- What environmental benefit can reasonably be attributed to device reuse or extended useful life?

### Intervention Feasibility

- What similar programs already exist?
- What outcomes have they reported?
- Which components appear scalable?
- What outcomes remain uncertain?

## Analytical Principle

Correlation will not be treated as proof of causation.

Published program outcomes will be distinguished from results generated directly through this capstone analysis.
